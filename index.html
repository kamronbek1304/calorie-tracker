Calorie Tracker

Privacy Policy

Effective 15 August 2026 Applies to the Calorie Tracker iOS app

This app records what you eat. That means it holds information about your body and your habits, so it is worth being precise about what is collected, where it goes, and how to get rid of it.

01 Who is responsible

Calorie Tracker is provided by Adizov Kamron. Questions about this policy, or about your data, go to adizovkamrom@gmail.com.

02 What is collected

Only what the app needs to work. Nothing is gathered in the background.

  • Account. Your email address if you register with one. If you use Sign in with Apple, Apple sends an identifier and, on first sign-in only, your name and an email address — which may be a private relay address that hides your real one.
  • Your profile. Name, age, gender, height, weight, goal, activity level, daily calorie target and any custom protein, carbohydrate and fat targets you set. You enter these during setup and can change them at any time.
  • Your meals. For each entry: the dish name, calories, protein, carbohydrates, fat, the time you ate, the meal type, and the photo if you took one.
  • A daily counter. How many AI estimates you have requested today, so free and subscribed accounts can be told apart.
There is no analytics or advertising software in this app. No third-party tracking libraries are installed, nothing follows you across other apps or websites, and your data is never sold.

03 How photos and descriptions are used

When you photograph a meal or describe it in writing, that photo or text is sent to OpenAI, which returns an estimate of the dish and its nutrition. The estimate is then yours to correct before you save it.

Photos you save are stored so they can appear next to the meal in your history. They are kept at web addresses that cannot be guessed, but anyone holding the exact address can open the image. Treat a meal photo the way you would treat any photo you have shared a link to.

04 Who else handles your data

Three companies process data on the app's behalf. There are no others.

Supabase

Runs the database, sign-in and file storage. Your account, profile, meals and photos live here.

OpenAI

Receives a meal photo or written description and returns the nutrition estimate. It receives the food, not your profile or your history.

Apple

Handles Sign in with Apple and every subscription payment. Card details go to Apple and are never seen by this app.

05 How long it is kept

Your data stays until you remove it. Delete an individual meal and it is gone along with its photo. Delete your account and everything goes: the account itself, every meal, every photo.

Account deletion lives inside the app, under Profile, and asks for your password before it proceeds. It cannot be undone, and there is no copy to restore from.

06 Your rights

  • See it. Everything the app holds about you is visible in the app itself.
  • Correct it. Profile details and any saved meal can be edited or removed at any time.
  • Erase it. Profile, then Delete Account.
  • Ask. For anything the app doesn't cover, write to adizovkamrom@gmail.com.

07 Children

Calorie Tracker is not intended for children under 13, and accounts should not be created for them. If you believe a child has registered, write to adizovkamrom@gmail.com and the account will be removed.

08 Changes to this policy

If what the app collects changes, this page changes with it and the effective date above is updated. Continuing to use the app after a change means you accept the revised policy.
