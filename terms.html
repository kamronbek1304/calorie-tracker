02 What the app does

You photograph a meal or describe it in writing, and the app estimates its calories and macronutrients. It keeps a history of what you logged and shows how it adds up against a daily target.

Estimates are estimates

Nutrition figures are produced by an AI model looking at a photo or a sentence. It cannot weigh your food, see what is under the surface, or know how it was cooked. Numbers will sometimes be wrong, occasionally by a lot. Correct them before saving when you know better.
